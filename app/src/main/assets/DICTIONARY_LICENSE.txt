Accepted-Word Policy for WordGrid:
1. Words range strictly from 2 to 13 uppercase English alphabetic letters (A-Z).
2. Contains standard nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and inflected forms valid in standard English word games.
3. Does not require internet access; offline lookup uses an in-memory prefix Trie for immediate, zero-latency validation.
